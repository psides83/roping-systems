# Event Profitability

Open Profitability from an event dashboard. Financial staff can record arena rental, cattle, labor, awards, or other expenses, with an optional note. An expense belongs to the whole event or one roping. Shared expenses are not automatically allocated to ropings. Removed expenses are excluded from totals but retained in the audit history.

Net retained uses collected entry fees minus earmarked added-money contributions, plus received sponsor/other funding and actual net fund allocations, minus full payout obligations and recorded expenses. It does not mistake unpaid awards, uncollected fees, pledged sponsor money, or reserved fund allocations for profit. Fund transfers are payout funding, not producer operating revenue.

The final retained figure is withheld until the relevant ropings are complete and payouts finalized. It is based on recorded expenses, not an accounting ledger or statement of physical cash on hand. Expenses do not have separate paid/unpaid statuses, receipts, or bank reconciliation in this first version. Financial closeout remains available to investigate discrepancies. Membership dues and fines are not event entry income.

Expenses can be edited and removed with optimistic revision checks. A roping with expenses must have those expenses reassigned to the event or removed before the roping is deleted. Expense and report data are private to financial staff; they are not exposed publicly or to ropers.
